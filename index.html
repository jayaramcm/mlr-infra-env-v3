About Us

Most brick suppliers make you wait for a truck. We skip the truck.

For large orders, we move our machinery to your site and cast the bricks or fencing panels right there - no bulk transport, no bottlenecks, no guessing when the load arrives.

Vision

CC bricks, precast fencing, and paver blocks are what we build today. What we're building toward is bigger: a full range of materials, made the same way - on-site, on your terms.

Since 2021, we've delivered 30+ projects - from factory quarters to hospitals to apartment complexes - with 10+ more ongoing and upcoming. Amaraja has already put that to the test. So can you, anywhere in India.

Standard Sizes

Moulds Ready, Available Right Now

Solid CC
Bricks

Dense, high-strength bricks for the load it needs to carry - load-bearing walls, foundations, and structures that can't afford to flex.

Fly Ash CC
Bricks

The sustainable choice, without sacrificing strength. Made with fly ash, keeping kilns and clay pits out, so you build greener without compromising performance.

Interlocking
CC Bricks

No mortar, no wasted days. The blocks lock into each other on-site, cutting both labour time and material cost versus a conventional mortared wall

Paver CC
Blocks

For driveways, walkways, and outdoor flooring that needs to take traffic. Built to handle vehicle load and foot traffic without cracking or shifting over time.

Every size above is available as standard. Need something non-standard for your specific architectural or structural requirements?
